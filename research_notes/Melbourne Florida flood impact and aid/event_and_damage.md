# Melbourne, Florida flood (November 2026): event identification and physical damage

**Method caveat (read first):** Every figure below comes from web-search result summaries/snippets retrieved 2026-11-25. Direct page fetches were blocked in this session (hosts such as wftv.com, clickorlando.com, wlrn.org, mynews13.com, cotality.com, watchers.news were outside the session's network allowlist), so no article was read in full and no figure was checked against the primary NWS / Brevard County / FEMA documents. ## 1. What was the event (name, dates, cause, rainfall, river levels, record status)?

### Takeaway
A 2026 event exists and is ongoing: the "2026 Fall Flooding" in east-central Florida, an unnamed (non-tropical) heavy-rain event in two rounds. Round 1 was Nov 1-2, 2026 (about 20-24.5 inches in southern Brevard: Malabar, Palm Bay, Melbourne Beach; Flash Flood Emergency for West Melbourne and Palm Bay; described as a 1-in-1,000-year rainfall). Round 2 was about Nov 17-20, 2026 (new flash flood warnings Nov 19, St. Johns River at Cocoa above its 1953 record). A federal major disaster declaration (FEMA-4956-DR) was signed Nov 24, 2026 with an incident period starting Oct 31, 2026 and continuing.

### Inferences
- The event has no storm name; "2026 Fall Flooding" (state) and FEMA-4956-DR are the usable official labels. Round 1 (Nov 1-2) is the flash-flood disaster for Melbourne/Palm Bay/West Melbourne; round 2 (Nov 17-20) added river flooding and re-flooded Palm Bay.
- The Malabar discrepancy (24.10 vs 24.52 in) and Palm Bay (19.75 / 20.60 / 22 in) likely reflect different gauges and cutoff times, not errors.
- Do not confuse with the Oct 26, 2025 north-Brevard flood (up to 15 in at Titusville/Port St. John/Merritt Island; 150-200 homes with water inside) — [Florida Realtors](https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2025/11/disaster-relief-fund-opens-after-flash-floods), [FOX 35](https://www.fox35orlando.com/news/brevard-lake-county-residents-impacted-flash-flood-flooded-homes-neighborhoods). Several search hits (and one WDBO Lamplighter Village URL filed under "lake-county") mix the two years.
