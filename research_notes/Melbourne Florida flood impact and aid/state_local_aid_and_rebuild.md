## 1. What is the event? (identification, scale, federal status)

### Takeaway
The event is the "2026 Fall Flooding" / November 2026 Florida floods: a flash-flood emergency on Nov 1-2, 2026 centered on Palm Bay / West Melbourne / Melbourne (southern Brevard), followed by a second heavy-rain wave about Nov 17-20 that hit central/north Brevard and pushed the St. Johns River to record levels. Federal major disaster declaration FEMA DR-4956 was approved Nov 24, 2026 with Individual Assistance for Brevard; home-damage counts are still self-reported and rising (869 to 2,000+ depending on source/date).

### Cited Findings
- Nov 2, 2026: county officials called the rainfall "borderline historic"; drivers and residents trapped; Brevard Public Schools closed two days; cities suspended trash pickup; hospitals postponed elective surgery — [ClickOrlando, 2026-11-02](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/11/02/borderline-historic-rainfall-floods-brevard-county-trapping-drivers-and-residents/)

### Inferences
- Realistic count of flooded homes in Brevard is somewhere between ~870 formally self-reported and 2,000+ (Governor's letter), before the second wave (Lake Poinsett/Cocoa/Rockledge/north Brevard) is added. Self-reports undercount; the final number will come from FEMA registrations.
- Two distinct geographies and timelines: (a) south Brevard flash flood (Palm Bay, West Melbourne, Melbourne) — water receded within days, demolition/rebuild already under way since ~Nov 9; (b) St. Johns River/central-north Brevard riverine flooding — homes still under water Nov 25, repair work there starts weeks to months later.
- An older, separate event exists (Oct 26, 2025 storms; SBA loans, deadlines Jan 6, 2026 physical) — do not confuse its SBA/aid pages with 2026.

## 2. State of Florida aid

### Takeaway
State aid actually confirmed for this event: emergency EO 26-227/26-228, a $10M Small Business Emergency Bridge Loan activation (0% interest, up to $100K, Brevard eligible, reported deadline Jan 1, 2027), state-run "muck-and-gut" crews (250+ properties), Multi-Agency Resource Centers, and SHIP housing dollars routed through cities. Statewide standing programs (property-tax refund under s. 197.319, Elevate Florida, My Safe Florida Home) exist but are slow, limited, or do not cover flood.

### Cited Findings
- **Executive orders:** EO 26-227 (Nov 2, 2026, seven counties incl. Brevard), amended by EO 26-228 (Nov 4); later expanded geographically. Allows Guard activation, road closures — [The Watchers](https://watchers.news/2026/11/03/florida-declares-emergency-after-catastrophic-flooding-in-southern-brevard-county/); [EO 26-228](https://www.flgov.com/eog/sites/default/files/executive-orders/2026/EO%2026-228.pdf)
- **Small Business Emergency Bridge Loan:** FloridaCommerce activated the program with $10 million for "2026 Fall Flooding"; 21 eligible counties incl. Brevard; up to $100,000 per small business (up to $150,000 agriculture/aquaculture); interest-free, short-term; described as personal loans, not grants; business must have been established before the event; apply at FloridaJobs.org/DisasterRecovery/EBL; business recovery line 833-832-4494 — [FDEM news release](https://www.floridadisaster.org/news-media/news/icymi-floridacommerce-activates-the-florida-small-business-emergency-bridge-loan-program-making-$10-million-available-for-businesses-impacted-by-the-2026-fall-flooding/); [Florida Realtors](https://www.floridarealtors.org/news-media/news-articles/2026/11/florida-opens-10-million-loan-program-flood-affected-businesses); [WFTV](https://www.wftv.com/news/local/volusia-county/florida-small-businesses-can-access-10-million-emergency-bridge-loans/HWGP3LFQUFAXTJCUUCVZLZMVGM/); [Brevard Storm Center](https://www.brevardfl.gov/StormCenter)
- Bridge loan application deadline reported as **January 1, 2027** (single local source; verify on FloridaCommerce page) — [Talk of Titusville, 2026-11-25](https://talkoftitusville.com/2026/11/25/fema-disaster-help-is-now-open-for-titusville-and-north-brevard/)
- **State muck-and-gut:** Governor's update: 250+ properties have received muck-and-gut services; two Florida State Guard teams doing muck-and-gut in Brevard and Okeechobee; FDOT deployed 750+ personnel, 100 pieces of heavy equipment, 45+ pumps — [flgov.com release](https://www.flgov.com/eog/news/press/2026/governor-ron-desantis-issues-updates-state-response-and-recovery-efforts-november); [FDEM ICYMI](https://www.floridadisaster.org/news-media/news/icymi-governor-ron-desantis-issues-updates-on-state-response-and-recovery-efforts-for-november-fall-flooding/)
- **Multi-Agency Resource Centers (MARCs):** Tony Rosa Community Center (Palm Bay) and West Melbourne Veterans Memorial Complex (Minton Rd); state update lists both as "closing 11/25"; county notice gave hours 8 a.m.-7 p.m. daily — [FDEM ICYMI](https://www.floridadisaster.org/news-media/news/icymi-governor-ron-desantis-issues-updates-on-state-response-and-recovery-efforts-for-november-fall-flooding/); [ClickOrlando, 2026-11-25](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/11/25/where-central-florida-flood-victims-can-find-help/)
- **Disaster Unemployment Assistance:** reported as included in FEMA DR-4956 designations for IA counties (administered by FloridaCommerce) — [Florida Phoenix, 2026-11-25](https://floridaphoenix.com/2026/11/25/disaster-declared-trump-approves-assistance-for-florida-floods/)
- **Property-tax refund, Fla. Stat. 197.319:** partial refund of property tax when a residential improvement is uninhabitable for at least 30 days due to a catastrophic event; file Form DR-465 with the county property appraiser by March 1 of the year after the event (i.e., March 1, 2027); appraiser issues determination by April 1; refund prorated by uninhabitable days (95 days = ~26% of year's tax) — [Statute](https://m.flsenate.gov/Statutes/197.319); [FL DOR FAQ (11/2025)](https://floridarevenue.com/property/Documents/catastrophic_event_tax_refunds.pdf); [Form DR-465](https://floridarevenue.com/property/Documents/dr465.pdf)
- **Elevate Florida (FDEM residential mitigation: elevation, reconstruction, acquisition):** slow. June 2026: ~20 homeowner projects approved over prior 6-8 weeks; July 2026: FEMA had provided ~$24 million; target 1,000-1,300 applicants; approved applicants still waiting; state says program "may be activated again in response to future natural disasters" — [Bay News 9, 2026-07-29](https://baynews9.com/fl/tampa/news/2026/07/29/elevate-florida-applicants-still-waiting-for-funding-answers); [Tampa Bay 28](https://www.tampabay28.com/news/region-pinellas/a-slow-drip-florida-official-says-elevate-florida-program-is-finally-starting-to-move); [FDEM Dec 2025 snapshot](https://www.floridadisaster.org/globalassets/dem/mitigation/elevate-florida/final---elevate-florida-snapshot_december-1.9.26-002.pdf)
- **My Safe Florida Home:** wind/hurricane mitigation only; flood barriers/panels "not currently eligible" (source is a vendor) — [Storm Smart](https://www.stormsmart.com/my-safe-florida-home-program/my-safe-florida-home-flood-protection/). Funding figures conflict across contractor blogs: ~$378.3M reappropriated in FY2026-27 incl. ~$342.6M for grants and "accepting applications as of Nov 2, 2026", vs ~$405M carried forward; grants up to $10,000, limited to low/moderate-income homeowners — [ArmorPro guide](https://www.armorprowindows.com/blog/my-safe-florida-home-program); [Guardian Shield](https://guardianshieldwindows.com/my-safe-florida-home-program-2026-funding-status/) (low-reliability vendor sources)
- **SHIP (State Housing Initiatives Partnership):** state housing funds being used locally under disaster strategies — see Section 3 (Palm Bay ~$1M) — [City of Palm Bay](https://www.palmbayfl.gov/Home/Components/News/News/13796)
- Politics/funding context: 2026 SB 7040 adds legislative oversight of the Emergency Preparedness and Response Fund; DeSantis signalled a possible special session — [FL Voice](https://flvoicenews.com/desantis-warns-florida-flooding-could-last-months-signals-possible-special-session-on-amendment-3/)

### Inferences
- For homeowners, the state offers almost no direct cash: the practical state benefits are free muck-out labor, SHIP repair money via the city (income-limited), and a prorated property-tax refund. The gap is filled by FEMA IA (just opened), insurance (many lack flood coverage) and out-of-pocket/financing — which means customers will be price-sensitive and financing/insurance-documentation help is a selling point.
- For the user's clients (electricians/plumbers who are small businesses in Brevard), the Bridge Loan is relevant only if they themselves suffered physical/economic injury.
- Elevate Florida is not a near-term work driver; it is a multi-year pipeline with federal cost-share and has not been reported as reopened for this event.

### Gaps
- No evidence found of: Florida Disaster Fund activation, Hope Florida deployment, D-SNAP approval, a disaster sales-tax holiday, or Hometown Heroes disaster funds for this event (searched; nothing returned). D-SNAP requires federal IA declaration, which only came Nov 24 — watch DCF.
- Elevate Florida cost share and whether it will open for DR-4956 not confirmed this session. Background knowledge, unverified this session: the 2025 round required homeowners to pay up to 25% of project cost.
- Bridge Loan repayment term not confirmed for this activation. Background knowledge, unverified: prior activations were 1-year, 0% loans.
- Exact MSFH current balance should be checked at mysafeflhome.com.

## 3. Brevard County and city help (Melbourne, Palm Bay, West Melbourne, Cocoa)

### Takeaway
Local help is concrete and repair-relevant: Palm Bay and West Melbourne waived building-permit fees for flood repairs, Palm Bay put ~$1M of SHIP into a disaster repair program that explicitly pays for emergency plumbing and electrical work, all jurisdictions ran flood-debris pickup from Nov 9-14, and the county runs a damage self-report (Crisis Track) form, a flood line (321-637-3818), and a free volunteer cleanup portal.

### Cited Findings
- **Palm Bay permit-fee waiver:** Building Department waiving permit fees for residential flood-related repairs; applicant must submit photos of flood damage with the permit application; properties in a flood zone "may not qualify" (per search summary) — [Palm Bay November Flood Event page](https://www.palmbayfl.gov/government/city-departments-f-to-z/fire-rescue-and-emergency-management/november-flood-event); [The Palm Bayer](https://palmbayer.com/news/palm-bay-flood-recovery-report-your-damage/). An older Palm Bay notice (not this event) said state surcharge and C&D charges still apply under fee waivers — [Palm Bay Building Dept (older)](https://www.palmbayfl.gov/Home/Components/News/News/10699/1319?arch=1)
- **West Melbourne permit-fee waiver:** City Council adopted legislation Nov 17, 2026 waiving building-permit fees for repairs required by the Nov 1-2, 2026 floods — [West Melbourne Emergency Information](https://westmelbourne.gov/905/Emergency-Information)
- **Palm Bay Disaster Relief Program (SHIP):** ~$1 million in unencumbered SHIP funds for low/moderate-income households; opened mid-Nov 2026. Eligible uses: insurance deductible, weatherproofing, water extraction, removal of water-damaged materials, **emergency plumbing or electrical work**, temporary structural stabilization. Rules: property in Palm Bay; owned at least 1 year with homestead exemption; mortgage, taxes, utilities current; assistance over $20,000 requires a lien (0% forgivable mortgage, 5 years). Apply via online prequalification at palmbayfl.gov/housing — [City of Palm Bay announcement](https://www.palmbayfl.gov/Home/Components/News/News/13796); [WESH](https://www.wesh.com/article/palm-bay-opens-disaster-relief-program-homeowners/74421476); [Palm Bayer council recap (Nov 5 meeting)](https://palmbayer.com/news/rcm-2026-11-05-recap/)
- **Palm Bay state of emergency:** declared by Mayor Rob Medina and City Manager Matthew Morton effective Nov 2, 2026 — [City of Palm Bay](https://www.palmbayfl.gov/Home/Components/News/News/13808/17)
- **Debris pickup start dates:** West Melbourne contractor Nov 9; unincorporated south Brevard Nov 10; City of Melbourne Nov 13 (city limits; sorting rules apply); Palm Bay citywide Nov 14, starting Northeast, two passes — [ClickOrlando, 2026-11-13](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/11/13/debris-pickup-begins-in-melbourne-after-flooding-here-are-the-rules/); [Palm Bay flood page](https://www.palmbayfl.gov/government/city-departments-f-to-z/fire-rescue-and-emergency-management/november-flood-event)

- County landfills closed for repairs, planned to resume Nov 23 — [Brevard Storm Center / state update](https://www.brevardfl.gov/StormCenter)
- **County damage reporting:** Crisis Track self-report form (not an aid application; feeds declaration requests); county flood-damage line 321-637-3818; 211 Brevard (2-1-1 or 321-632-6688) — [Talk of Titusville](https://talkoftitusville.com/2026/11/25/fema-disaster-help-is-now-open-for-titusville-and-north-brevard/); [Brevard Storm Center](https://www.brevardfl.gov/StormCenter)

### Inferences
- The Palm Bay SHIP program is a direct, named funding source for electrician and plumber invoices ("emergency plumbing or electrical work"); contractors who can work with the city's SHIP vendor/payment process have an edge. $1M is small (~50 homes at $20K each).
- Permit-fee waivers lower the cost of doing permitted (legal) work and remove an excuse for skipping permits; marketing can say "permit fees waived in Palm Bay and West Melbourne for flood repairs."
- The affected housing mix includes older slab-on-grade Palm Bay (General Development-era) homes, mobile-home parks (Lamplighter Village) and apartments — different electrical/plumbing scopes and different payers (owners, park operators, landlords).

- City of Melbourne: no permit-fee waiver, utility-bill relief, or emergency repair grant found. Brevard County (unincorporated) permit-fee waiver and county SHIP disaster strategy not found. Cocoa/Rockledge/Titusville measures not found.
- Per-household cap for Palm Bay SHIP not found. Income limits not found (SHIP is normally capped at 120-140% AMI — background knowledge, unverified).
- No utility bill forgiveness/late-fee waiver found for any city.

## 5. Causes officials cite, and long-term fixes planned or funded

### Takeaway
Officials blame rainfall beyond design capacity plus canal limits: the Melbourne-Tillman canal system "filled, then flowed backward," canals could not be pre-lowered in time, and by the second wave the whole county was saturated. No post-flood long-term fix (buyouts, stormwater bond, Resilient Florida award) has been announced; pre-existing shortfalls are large (county $2.4B infrastructure gap; Palm Bay ~$9M/yr stormwater fee vs. needs), and Palm Bay's road/bridge repair bill alone is $10-15M.

### Cited Findings
- Palm Bay Public Works Director Kevin Brinkley told council the Melbourne-Tillman canal system filled, then flowed backward; four neighborhood canal crossings failed and remain closed; restoring roads and bridges will cost $10-15 million — [Palm Bayer council recap, Nov 5, 2026 meeting](https://palmbayer.com/news/rcm-2026-11-05-recap/)

## 6. Rebuilding rules that drive electrical and plumbing work

### Takeaway
Utility and industry rules force licensed trade work after flooding: FPL will not reconnect customer-side damaged equipment until a licensed electrician repairs it and an inspection passes; NEMA guidance says submerged breakers, receptacles/wiring devices, GFCI/AFCI and dry-location cable (NM-B) must be replaced, not dried out. Local permits are required (fees waived in Palm Bay/West Melbourne).

### Cited Findings
- **FPL:** FPL owns the service line and meter; if customer equipment is damaged, repairs are needed before FPL can connect service; hire a licensed electrician (unlicensed work can delay connection); after repairs and "a passed inspection has been received by FPL," the electrician calls FPL at 888-488-1624 to schedule reconnection (a second FPL sheet lists 800-226-3545 and requires the electrician's license number — verify which is current) — [FPL "Ready to safely receive power"](https://www.fpl.com/content/dam/fplgp/us/en/storm/pdf/Ready_to_safely_receive_power.pdf); [FPL customer repair fact sheet](https://www.fpl.com/content/dam/fplgp/us/en/reliability/pdf/customer-repair-fact-sheet.pdf)
- FPL flood safety: keep power off until a licensed electrician inspects the electrical system — [FPL "Please stay safe after the storm"](https://www.fpl.com/storm/pdf/PleaseStaySafeAfterTheStorm.pdf)
- FPL outages during this event ran from Titusville to Palm Bay, some from damaged equipment — [WESH](https://www.wesh.com/article/power-outage-brevard-county-rain-flooding/74311712)
- **NEMA "Evaluating Water-Damaged Electrical Equipment"** (GD 1-2019; now reissued as NEMA CS 70006-2026): table marks molded-case circuit breakers, wiring devices (receptacles/switches), GFCI/AFCI and other electronic devices, and wire/cable listed for dry locations (e.g., NM-B) as "replace"; wet-location cable may be reconditioned only if undamaged and ends not exposed — [NEMA GD 1-2019](https://www.nema.org/docs/default-source/standards-document-library/nema-gd-1-2019-evaluating-water-damaged-electrical-equipment-guide.pdf); [NEMA CS 70006-2026 copy (IEC)](https://ieci.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/NEMA-CS-70006-2026-Evaluating-Water-Damaged-Electrical-Equipment-GD-1.pdf); [NEMA storm FAQs](https://nema.org/storm-disaster-recovery/storm-faqs); [NEMA replacing/upgrading page](https://www.nema.org/storm-disaster-recovery/replacing-and-relocating-equipment/replacing-or-upgrading-water-damaged-electrical-equipment)
- Manufacturer position: electrical equipment that has been submerged or contacted water needs to be replaced — [Schneider Electric storm FAQ](https://se.com/us/en/work/services/water-damaged-electrical-equipment/storm-response-faq)
- County emergency management advised residents to confirm contractors pull required building permits — [ClickOrlando, 2026-11-06](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/11/06/how-do-you-know-who-to-trust-palm-bay-flood-victims-warned-about-predatory-contractors/)
- Sewage backups into homes during the event (sanitary sewer/lateral issue) — [FOX 35](https://www.fox35orlando.com/video/fmc-disfh6czd8cd3mca)

### Inferences
- In a typical slab-on-grade home with 6-24 inches of water, NEMA-driven scope = every receptacle and device box below the waterline, the NM-B feeding them, any low-mounted sub-panel/disconnect, AC condenser disconnect/whip, and water-heater/appliance connections; main panels (usually ~4-5 ft high) survive unless water was deep. Mobile homes (Lamplighter Village) have under-floor wiring and plumbing that floods at shallower depths.
- Reconnection requirement = guaranteed first-call demand for electricians where meters/panels were wet; permit + inspection is a gate before drywall, so electricians/plumbers are early in the rebuild sequence (weeks 2-10 after water recedes).
- Substantial-damage (50%) determinations in mapped flood zones may be why Palm Bay says flood-zone properties "may not qualify" for the simple fee waiver — those need floodplain review.

### Gaps
- Nothing found locally for this event on the 50% substantial damage/improvement rule, floodplain ordinance text, or any substantial-damage letters issued by Palm Bay, Melbourne, West Melbourne or the county.
- Background knowledge, unverified this session: NFIP communities must require structures in the Special Flood Hazard Area damaged to 50%+ of pre-damage market value to be brought into compliance (elevated to base flood elevation plus freeboard; Florida Building Code requires BFE+1 ft and elevation of electrical, plumbing and mechanical equipment); Florida Building Code/NEC requires permits for panel/service/wiring replacement and water-heater replacement; flooded private wells should be shock-chlorinated and tested and flooded septic systems inspected (Florida DOH guidance). None of these were sourced this session — verify before use.
- Septic/well prevalence in the flooded Palm Bay areas (large parts of Palm Bay are on septic and wells — background knowledge) not sourced.

## 8. Contractor rules during the declared emergency

### Takeaway
A Governor-declared emergency (EO 26-227, in force since Nov 2, 2026) makes unlicensed contracting a third-degree felony under s. 489.127, and officials are actively warning residents about predatory/unlicensed contractors — so licensed status, permits, written scopes and no-pressure sales are both legal requirements and the marketing message.

### Cited Findings
- Fla. Stat. 489.127(2)(c): an unlicensed person who violates the contracting prohibitions "during the existence of a state of emergency declared by executive order of the Governor commits a felony of the third degree" — [Florida Statutes 489.127](https://m.flsenate.gov/statutes/489.127)
- Local practice example (Charlotte County after Ian): unlicensed work during a state of emergency may be charged as a felony; contractors must register with the county before contracting — [Charlotte County](https://www.charlottecountyfl.gov/departments/community-development/notices/unlicensed-contracting-is-a-felony.stml)
- Nov 6, 2026: Brevard County Emergency Management warned Palm Bay flood victims about predatory contractors — unsolicited offers, high-pressure tactics, large upfront payments; advised researching contractors, asking neighbors, confirming building permits — [ClickOrlando, 2026-11-06](https://www.clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/11/06/how-do-you-know-who-to-trust-palm-bay-flood-victims-warned-about-predatory-contractors/)
- Nov 24, 2026: renewed warning to avoid unlicensed contractors — get scope, total price and completion date in writing; pressure to sign or pay immediately is a red flag — [WFTV, 2026-11-24](https://www.wftv.com/news/local/homeowners-warned-avoid-unlicensed-contractors-after-recent-flooding/4POEGFAYCRDZRK62IIVXOLNBSQ/)
- FPL requires the electrician's license number to schedule reconnection — [FPL customer repair fact sheet](https://www.fpl.com/content/dam/fplgp/us/en/reliability/pdf/customer-repair-fact-sheet.pdf)

### Inferences
- Residents are being told by the county and TV news to distrust door-knockers and demand licenses, permits and written scopes. Effective, compliant marketing for electricians/plumbers here leads with license number, local address, permit handling ("fees waived"), written fixed-scope estimates, FPL-reconnect know-how, and help documenting for FEMA/insurance/SHIP — and avoids door-to-door pressure and "flood special" urgency pricing.
- Out-of-area contractors face local registration/competency requirements; local licensed firms have a credibility advantage.

### Gaps
- No Attorney General price-gouging hotline activation notice or DBPR emergency order found for this event (searched). 
- Background knowledge, unverified this session — verify at flsenate.gov / myfloridalegal.com / myfloridalicense.com before use: (a) s. 501.160 bars unconscionable prices for essential commodities (incl. repair services/materials) during a declared emergency, civil penalties up to $1,000 per violation and $25,000 per 24 hours, AG hotline 1-866-9NO-SCAM; (b) assignment of benefits for residential/commercial property insurance policies issued on or after Jan 1, 2023 is prohibited (SB 2-A, 2022; s. 627.7152); (c) s. 489.147 restricts contractor solicitation/inducements around insurance claims; (d) s. 489.103/489.105 require state certification or local registration for electrical (EC/ER) and plumbing (CFC) contractors; (e) home-solicitation sales carry a 3-day cancellation right (s. 501.021-.055); contracts signed within a declared emergency for storm-damaged property carry extended cancellation rights under s. 489.147. None confirmed by search this session.
