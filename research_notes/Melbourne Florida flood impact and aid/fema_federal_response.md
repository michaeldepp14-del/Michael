## 1. What is the event, and what is the declaration status (federal and state)?

### Takeaway
The event is the "November Fall Flooding": a record rain event on Nov. 1–3, 2026 (about 24 inches near Malabar in south Brevard, flooding Melbourne/Palm Bay), followed by a second round Nov. 17–20 that pushed the St. Johns River past its record. President Trump approved a major disaster declaration, FEMA DR-4956, on Tuesday Nov. 24, 2026 — 15 days after the state's Nov. 9 request — and Brevard is designated for BOTH Individual Assistance and Public Assistance. A second request for the Nov. 17–20 flooding is still pending.

## 6. Changes to FEMA in 2025–2026 that affect speed and size of help

### Takeaway
Declarations now take far longer than they historically did, and FEMA's future structure is in flux. Brevard's 15-day wait was fast by current standards, but the declaration came with narrow PA and no HMGP, and the Review Council has recommended higher thresholds and a lower federal cost share.

### Cited Findings
- Major disaster declarations have averaged nearly 10 weeks in the current administration versus three weeks or less over the prior two decades (independent review); an AP analysis in July found about a month and a half on average — [The Conversation / WLRN, 2026-10-14](https://www.wlrn.org/environment/2026-10-14/how-trumps-disaster-aid-delays-and-fema-cuts-could-galvanize-the-movable-middle-in-the-2026-elections)
- 2026 Texas floods: some counties approved after a seven-week delay — [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/how-trumps-disaster-aid-delays-and-fema-cuts-could-galvanize-the-movable-middle-in-the-2026-elections-294236)
- A plan to cut FEMA staffing by 50% was found unlawful by a judge in September 2026 — [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/how-trumps-disaster-aid-delays-and-fema-cuts-could-galvanize-the-movable-middle-in-the-2026-elections-294236)
- FEMA Review Council final report (released May 7, 2026): ten recommendations shifting responsibility to states; replace PA with a "RAPID" model tied to predefined criteria and estimated costs; replace IA and HMGP; raise the threshold for federal assistance; lower federal cost share from 75–100% to 50–75%; reform the NFIP; phase in over two to three years; the 50% staffing cut was dropped from the final report — [NACo](https://www.naco.org/news/fema-review-council-releases-final-report-recommending-sweeping-changes); [NLC, 2026-05-14](https://www.nlc.org/article/2026/05/14/fema-review-council-final-report-signals-major-shift-in-federal-disaster-policy/); [Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/agency-oversight/2026/05/fema-review-council-backs-off-on-staffing-cuts-in-final-report/)
- Most of those changes need legislation; declaration criteria can be changed by executive action — [Bipartisan Policy Center](https://bipartisanpolicy.org/issue-brief/fema-reform-comparing-the-review-councils-recommendations-and-congressional-proposals/); [CRS IN12693](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/IN12693)
- Local effect: Florida's emergency management director publicly responded to FEMA delays while Jensen Beach victims waited; a Florida mayor posted a viral video pleading with the President; an official said a new FEMA director was expected to take office Nov. 30 — [WFLX, 2026-11-21](https://www.wflx.com/2026/11/21/florida-emergency-management-director-responds-fema-delays-jensen-beach-flood-victims-wait-help/); [ClickOrlando, 2026-11-24](https://clickorlando.com/news/local/2026/11/24/mayor-of-flood-ravaged-florida-city-begs-trump-for-fema-help-in-viral-facebook-video)
- Residents waited without federal help for three weeks, through a second flood — [Florida Today via Yahoo, "Brevard is flooding again. Where is FEMA?"](https://www.yahoo.com/news/weather-news/articles/brevard-flooding-again-where-fema-165036028.html)

### Inferences
- Nov. 9 request to Nov. 24 approval is 15 days — quicker than the reported 6–10 week average, plausibly helped by congressional pressure and the second flood.
- The narrow package (PA Categories A–B only, HMGP under review, fewer counties than requested) fits the pattern of smaller federal commitments and more cost left with the state and county.

### Gaps
- No source found on whether any Review Council recommendation (thresholds, cost share, block grants) has actually been implemented as of November 2026, or on the identity of the incoming FEMA head.
- No data on FEMA staffing levels or inspector availability in Florida for DR-4956.
